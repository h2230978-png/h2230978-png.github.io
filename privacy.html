Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Your business data stays on your PC

Products, bills, customers, stock and reports are stored on the computer where BuildStock is installed. We do not receive them.

What we receive

When you buy: your name, shop name, mobile number, email and your PC's request code, so we can issue and support your licence.

When you use "Report a problem": only if you send a report, BuildStock sends us its version, your licence ID, your Windows version, the recent error log and your note. Reports contain no bills, customer lists or passwords.

Updates: BuildStock checks for new versions on our download site. This sends no business data.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment partner. We receive confirmation of your payment, never your card or bank details.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only when the law requires it or our payment partner needs it to process your payment.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your contact details for as long as you use your licence and for our tax and accounting records. To see or delete them, contact us using the details on our Contact page.
