Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Your business data stays on your PC

Products, bills, customers, stock and reports are stored on the computer where BuildStock is installed. We do not receive them.

What we receive

When you buy: your name, shop name, mobile number, email and your PC's request code, so we can issue and support your licence.

When you use "Report a problem": only if you send a report, BuildStock sends us its version, your licence ID, your Windows version, the recent error log and your note. Reports contain no bills, customer lists or passwords.

Updates: BuildStock checks for new versions on our download site. This sends no business data.

Why we use it

To make and send your activation code, to help you when you ask for support, to replace your licence if your PC changes, and to keep the records the law requires for sales and tax. We use your mobile number to send your activation code and support replies on WhatsApp.

Where it is kept

Your order is kept in our payment partner's dashboard and in our own sales records. Problem reports and our sales records are kept in our Google account (Gmail and Google Sheets). Activation codes and support replies are sent through WhatsApp.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment partner. We receive confirmation of your payment, never your card or bank details.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only when the law requires it or our payment partner needs it to process your payment.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your contact details for as long as you use your licence and for as long as tax and accounting law requires us to keep sales records. After that we delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to delete them (except what the law requires us to keep). You can also ask us to stop messaging you on WhatsApp. Write to us using the contact below; we reply within 30 days.

Questions and complaints

Contact BuildStock at help.buildstock@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +91 76206 10314. We aim to resolve a complaint within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

BuildStock is a business product for shop owners and is not meant for children.
