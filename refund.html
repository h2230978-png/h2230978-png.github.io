Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Try before you buy

BuildStock has a free 7-day trial with every feature, so you can check it suits your shop before paying.

Before your activation code is sent

You can cancel your order and get a full refund. Message us with your payment reference.

After your activation code is sent

The licence is digital and tied to your PC, so it cannot be refunded once we have sent the activation code.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you were charged twice, or money left your account but the payment failed, we refund the extra amount in full.

If we cannot deliver

If we have not sent your activation code within 3 days of payment, you can ask for a full refund.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after we approve them.
